Projects · Aviation · Heathrow

Menzies Aviation

A consistent Microsoft Teams Rooms experience across three meeting rooms and two private offices at Menzies Aviation’s Heathrow operation.

Location
Heathrow, London
Type
Microsoft Teams Rooms, multi-room AV
Services
Audio Visual

The brief

Menzies Aviation – the leading service partner to the world’s airports and airlines – needed to improve communication and collaboration across departments at its fast-paced Heathrow office. Its existing set-up relied on basic AV that was inadequate for its growing needs, and it needed flexible spaces for both small meetings and larger team sessions.

What we delivered

BMT designed and built a multi-room, Microsoft Teams-based solution with 21:9 ultra-wide screens and room booking: Yealink MVC86 and S90 multi-camera systems, Nureva HDL310 audio, Jupiter 105″ and 81″ ultra-wide displays, Yealink A40 video bars, consistent Teams interfaces, intuitive control panels and Yealink Room Booking – plus staff training and ongoing support.

The result

A high-quality, user-friendly AV ecosystem with the same Teams experience in every room. In the client’s words, it has “transformed meeting participation from a routine task into an engaging experience that users actively seek out.”

The AV solution BMT delivered has significantly enhanced our team communication dynamics. It has transformed meeting participation from a routine task into an engaging experience that users actively seek out.
Noel V. Pereira, Operational Standards Manager – Cargo, Menzies Aviation
