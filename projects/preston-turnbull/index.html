Preston Turnbull

Microsoft Teams Rooms, interactive displays and room booking for a fast-paced professional services firm.

Type
AV design and installation
Services
Audio Visual
Client

The brief

Preston Turnbull, a leading legal and advisory firm in a fast-paced, client-focused environment, was held back by outdated AV that limited communication and flexibility. It needed smart room booking, signage and Teams-based conferencing to support hybrid collaboration.

What we delivered

A comprehensive AV design tailored for productivity, including Microsoft Teams Rooms, interactive displays and video conferencing tools, with thorough user training.

The result

Better internal teamwork and client meetings. In the client’s words, the integration was seamless and the touchscreen functionality has really enhanced collaboration during meetings.

The BMT solution has significantly improved our team’s productivity. The integration was seamless and has already had a positive impact on our operations. The support provided was exceptional, with quick responses to all our requirements, ensuring everything was addressed efficiently.
Billy White, Events Manager, Preston Turnbull
