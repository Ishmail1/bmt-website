VR Capital

Audio visual design and installation across meeting rooms and boardrooms, built around a high-performance Zoom collaboration experience.

Type
Audio Visual Installation
Services
Audio Visual

The brief

VR Capital, in the financial services sector, wanted a high-performance, Zoom-based collaboration experience across its office – supporting seamless communication, streamlined meetings and interactive presentations, while keeping a modern, professional aesthetic.

What we delivered

BMT designed and installed a tailored AV solution across meeting rooms and boardrooms: a Jupiter 81″ 21:9 ultra-wide screen for immersive presentations; a Yealink ZVC conferencing system with Yealink 4K UVC86 cameras for intelligent framing and speaker tracking; Nureva HDL audio for consistent coverage wherever people sit; a 75″ iiyama touchscreen for whiteboarding and content sharing; LG digital signage; iPad-based room control; and fully integrated Zoom Rooms.

The result

A future-ready AV environment that enhances collaboration, supports hybrid working and positions the workplace as a hub for seamless, professional communication.
