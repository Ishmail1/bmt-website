Privacy Policy

Who we are

This website is operated by Business Movers Technology ("BMT", "we", "us").

  • Trading address: 1st Floor, 1 Bastwick Street, London, EC1V 3NU.
  • We are the data controller for the personal data described in this policy.

What we collect

  • Enquiries: details you give us through our contact form, by email or by phone, such as your name, email address, phone number, company and message.
  • Website usage: technical data such as IP address and browser type, which our website host and the third-party services described under Cookies below process when you visit.
  • Analytics: if you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics collects information about how you use our website, such as the pages you view, how you arrived, your approximate location, and your device and browser. See Cookies below.

How and why we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and provide quotes or consultations. Lawful basis: our legitimate interests, or steps taken at your request before entering into a contract.
  • To deliver and support services we have agreed to provide. Lawful basis: contract.
  • To run, secure and improve our website. Lawful basis: legitimate interests.
  • To understand how visitors find and use our website, using Google Analytics, only if you accept analytics cookies. Lawful basis: consent.
  • To send occasional updates, only where you have opted in. You can unsubscribe at any time. Lawful basis: consent.

Sharing

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We share it only with service providers who help us operate, such as our website host, our contact form provider, our email and IT providers, and Google (for website analytics, only if you accept analytics cookies), and only where necessary.
  • We may also share it where the law requires us to.
  • Some providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, we rely on appropriate safeguards, such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or adequacy regulations.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry data only for as long as we need it to deal with your enquiry, and client records for as long as required for contractual, tax and legal purposes. Google Analytics data is kept for 14 months and then deleted automatically.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • access your personal data
  • correct or erase it
  • restrict or object to its processing
  • data portability
  • withdraw your consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us: via our contact page or call 020 3909 8200. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113).

Cookies

  • Analytics cookies (only with your consent): we use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors find and use our website: the pages viewed, how visitors arrived (for example from a search engine or another website), approximate location (such as country or city) and device and browser type. Google acts as our processor.
  • These cookies are set only if you select Accept in our cookie banner. Before you choose, or if you select Reject, no analytics cookies are set on your device and Google Analytics receives only basic signals without cookies or identifiers.
  • The analytics cookies are _ga, which distinguishes visitors, and _ga_*, which keeps track of a visit (session). They expire after up to 2 years. Google Analytics data is kept for 14 months.
  • We do not use advertising or marketing cookies. Google Analytics advertising features, including Google signals and ad personalisation, are turned off.
  • We remember your choice in your browser's local storage (bmt_consent), so we don't ask you again on every page. You can change it at any time with the link at the bottom of every page.
  • If you send us a message through our contact form, we store only the times it was sent in your browser's local storage (bmt_form_sent) for 24 hours, to limit spam. It contains no personal details.
  • The map on our contact page is embedded from Google Maps, which may collect technical data, such as your IP address, under Google's own privacy policy. Our web fonts are hosted on this website, so no font requests go to third parties.
  • You can manage cookies through your browser settings.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.
