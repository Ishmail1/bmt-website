About Us

Smarter, better-connected workplaces.

Who we are

A trusted partner for workplace technology

At Business Movers Technology, we help organisations create smarter, better-connected workplaces. We design, deliver and support technology solutions that make collaboration easier, improve productivity and provide the reliable infrastructure businesses depend on every day.

With decades of combined industry experience, our team works closely with clients to understand their goals and deliver technology that is simple to use, scalable and built for the future.

From a single meeting room to a complete office fit-out, we provide a fully managed service, from consultancy and design through to installation, project management, training and ongoing support.

Whether you're relocating, refurbishing, expanding or upgrading your workplace technology, Business Movers Technology provides a single trusted partner to manage every stage of your project. We work with property companies, landlords, fit-out specialists, project managers, architects and businesses across the UK, helping them create modern, connected workspaces that people enjoy using.

Our mission is simple: to deliver technology that enables people to communicate better, collaborate more effectively and work smarter.

What we do

We bring together the technologies that modern businesses need, including:

  • Microsoft Teams and Zoom meeting rooms
  • IT Support & Managed Services
  • Audio Visual Solutions
  • CCTV & Access Control
  • Enterprise Internet Connectivity
  • Digital Signage
  • Structured Cabling & Network Infrastructure
  • Room Booking & Workplace Technology
  • Wi-Fi Design & Heatmapping
  • Acoustic Solutions & Sound Management
Why choose BMT?

Technology should empower people, not create complexity.

That's why we focus on delivering solutions that are intuitive, reliable and tailored to the way your business works. Every project is managed by experienced specialists who take pride in delivering exceptional service, clear communication and high-quality workmanship.

Our clients value us because we are:

  • Independent technology experts
  • Experienced across commercial offices of all sizes
  • Focused on quality and long-term partnerships
  • Responsive, proactive and easy to work with
  • Committed to delivering projects on time and within budget
