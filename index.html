Our process

How we work

One partner from first conversation to day-one go-live and beyond.

01

Survey

We start on site, or from the plans if the walls aren't built yet, to understand your spaces, people and goals.

02

Design

We design consistent, easy-to-use rooms and resilient connectivity, choosing the right platform and products for how you work.

03

Install

Our engineers install, test and project-manage the work around your programme, so you're live and ready on completion day.

04

Support

Training at handover, then ongoing support: remote help, onsite engineering, firmware and security updates, and warranty management.
