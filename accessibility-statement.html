Accessibility Statement

Business Movers Technology wants everyone to be able to use this website. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

What we do

  • Use clear headings, readable text and sufficient colour contrast.
  • Provide text alternatives for images.
  • Support keyboard navigation and visible focus states.
  • Label form fields clearly.
  • Respect reduced-motion settings for animations.

Known limitations

  • Some project photography and embedded content, such as maps and videos, may not be fully accessible.
  • We are working to fix these.
